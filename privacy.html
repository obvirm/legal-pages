Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-17

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when connecting your social media accounts, including access tokens and account identifiers. We also collect video content you upload for publishing.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information solely to provide the service: publishing content to your connected social media accounts. We do not sell, share, or use your data for any other purpose.

3. Data Storage

Access tokens are stored securely and used only to authenticate with social media platforms. Video content is processed temporarily and not stored longer than necessary for publishing.

4. Third-Party Services

This service interacts with TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram APIs. Your data is subject to their respective privacy policies when transmitted to these platforms.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data only while your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

6. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data, including encrypted storage of access tokens.

7. Your Rights

You may revoke access tokens at any time through your social media account settings. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

8. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact the service administrator.
